TAVERN TAPPER

Pixel Bar Rush - Game Instructions

Game Description

Tavern Tapper is a pixel-art bartending game where you play as a bartender serving drinks to customers. Your goal is to complete orders by mixing cocktails, pouring beer, and serving drinks within the time limit. The game features multiple levels with increasing difficulty, each requiring you to serve a certain number of drinks.

You'll progress through three levels per run: starting with difficulty 1, then difficulty 2, and finally difficulty 3. Complete all three levels to win the game!

How to Play

Basic Controls:

  • Fridge: Click the fridge icon to open the ingredients menu
  • Add Ingredients: Click on an ingredient to add it to the shaker, or drag it to the shaker
  • Shake: Drag the shaker around to shake it. The speed and rotation change based on your movement
  • Serve: Click the "Serve" button when the drink is ready
  • Beer: Click the beer tap to fill the glass, then drag it to the tray
  • Hint: Click the "Hint" button to see a hint. Click again quickly to see the solution
  • Pause: Click "Pause" to pause the game and access the menu
  • Restart: Click "Restart" to start a new game from the beginning

Game Mechanics:

  • Each level has a time limit and a target number of drinks to serve
  • Customers will appear with orders. Complete their orders to progress
  • For cocktails: Add ingredients to the shaker, shake it, then serve
  • For beer: Fill the glass from the tap and place it on the serving tray
  • Your best time for each level is tracked and displayed

📱 Mobile Instructions

On mobile devices, you can tap and drag ingredients and the shaker just like on desktop. The game is fully touch-optimized. Use your finger to drag the shaker around to shake cocktails, and tap ingredients to add them to the shaker. All controls work with touch gestures.

Tips & Strategies

  • Read the hints carefully - they tell you what ingredients are needed
  • Shake the shaker vigorously for better results
  • Pay attention to the order details in the speech bubble
  • Use the "Recipes" button to see all available cocktail recipes
  • Manage your time wisely - each level has a time limit
  • Practice makes perfect - try to beat your best time!

Game Menu

You can access the game menu at any time by clicking the "Pause" button. From the menu, you can:

  • Select different levels to play
  • Restart the current level
  • Resume the game

You can also restart the entire game at any time using the "Restart" button in the top bar, which will start a new run from difficulty 1.
